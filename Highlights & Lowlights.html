Aftercare & maintenance

  • Use color‑safe, sulfate‑free shampoo/conditioner.
  • Purple/blue toning once weekly to control brassiness.
  • Heat protectant before styling; limit heat to preserve tone.
  • Gloss/toner refresh every 6–8 weeks for vibrancy.

FAQ

Partial vs full foils — what’s the difference?
Partial focuses on the top and sides for a sun‑kissed effect. Full covers the entire head for maximum brightness and dimension throughout.
Will highlights damage my hair?
We use protective lighteners and bond‑building add‑ons. We’ll strand‑test and adjust developer strength for the healthiest lift.
How often should I refresh?
Most clients refresh partial foils every 8–12 weeks; full foils typically 12–16 weeks. Toner/gloss can be refreshed sooner.
