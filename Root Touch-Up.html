Aftercare & longevity

  • Wash 2–3× weekly with color‑safe shampoo/conditioner.
  • Protect from heat and UV; use heat protectant and UV spray.
  • Gloss/toner refresh every 4–6 weeks to keep tone perfect.
  • Book touch‑ups on a cadence that matches your growth pattern.

FAQ

Will the touch‑up match my current color?
Yes — we custom‑blend your formula to your existing shade and adjust for seasonal tone shifts (warmer/cooler) if desired.
Can I add highlights at the same visit?
Absolutely — many clients add a few foils or a face‑frame with a root service. Timing and price will be adjusted accordingly.
Does root touch‑up work on previously lightened hair?
For blondes, a root melt/shadow blends natural regrowth into light ends. We’ll tone the mid‑lengths/ends to keep everything cohesive.
