Tips for parents

  • Book a time when your child is usually rested and fed.
  • Bring a small snack, toy, or story. Screens are welcome for focus.
  • Dress in easy‑to‑shake‑off clothing; we provide capes and gentle neck tape.
  • If your child is anxious, mention it when booking — we’ll plan extra time.

FAQ

Can I stay with my child?
Absolutely. You’re welcome to sit beside them or hold them (for toddlers). Safety first.
Do you keep a lock of hair for first cuts?
Yes — ask your stylist and we’ll prepare a small keepsake envelope.
My child is sensitive to sound — is that ok?
Yes. We can avoid clippers, lower music, and book a quieter time slot. Noise‑reducing headphones are welcome.
