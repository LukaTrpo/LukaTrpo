Home care & styling tips

  • Use a heat protectant and finish with light hair oil on the ends.
  • For volume: blow‑dry with a round brush, then set with cool air.
  • Curly/wavy: apply curl cream on damp hair, diffuse at low heat.
  • Schedule trims every 6–10 weeks to maintain shape and healthy ends.

FAQ

How do I choose the right length?
We’ll consider face shape, hair density, styling time, and your wardrobe vibe to select a length and outline that flatters you.
Do you cut very fine or very thick hair?
Yes — techniques vary. For fine hair we protect weight at the perimeter; for thick hair we debulk internally for floaty movement.
Can I combine with coloring?
Absolutely. Many clients book coloring followed by a styling cut for a seamless finish. We’ll advise on order and timing.
