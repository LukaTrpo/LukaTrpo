Aftercare & longevity

  • Use sulfate‑free shampoo/conditioner; wash 2–3× per week.
  • Protect color from heat: apply heat protectant before styling.
  • Add a weekly color‑safe mask for softness and shine.
  • Book a gloss/toner refresh every 4–6 weeks to maintain tone.

FAQ

Will full color damage my hair?
We use premium, low‑ammonia formulas and add bonding/conditioning steps. A strand test helps us pick the gentlest approach.
How often do I need a refresh?
Typically every 5–8 weeks depending on growth and shade depth. We’ll suggest a personalized plan.
Can I go lighter with full color?
Permanent color lifts slightly, but significant lightening requires pre‑lightening/bleach + toner. We’ll advise on the cleanest route.
