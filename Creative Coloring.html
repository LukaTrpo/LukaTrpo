Aftercare & longevity

  • Wash 1–2× weekly with cool water; sulfate‑free shampoo/conditioner.
  • Use color‑depositing conditioners to refresh between visits.
  • Avoid chlorine and saltwater; use leave‑in UV protectant.
  • Book refreshes every 4–6 weeks for vivids; 6–8 for deeper tones.

FAQ

Do I need to bleach my hair for pastels?
Usually yes — pastels require a light, even base. We’ll assess with a strand test and keep the process as gentle as possible.
How long do vivids last?
Vivid shades are semi‑permanent; expect 3–6 weeks of peak vibrancy with proper care. We’ll send you home with a plan.
Will this damage my hair?
We prioritize hair health: targeted lightening, bond‑builders, low heat, and pro aftercare recommendations to keep hair strong and shiny.
