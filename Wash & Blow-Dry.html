Fresh finishes

Home care & longevity tips

  • Use a satin pillowcase to reduce frizz and preserve smoothness.
  • Refresh roots with cool air and a round brush the next day.
  • Apply a pea‑size serum to ends only; avoid over‑oiling.
  • For waves, twist sections and mist with light hold spray.

FAQ

How long will my blow‑dry last?
Typically 2–3 days depending on hair type, humidity and your routine. We’ll recommend products to extend longevity.
Do you work with curly hair?

Absolutely. Add a moisture mask, gloss, or bonding treatment for extra shine and strength. Great before events.
