Balayage vs Ombre

Balayage
  • Hand‑painted strands for sun‑kissed, natural brightness
  • Soft transition from roots with dimension around the face
  • Low maintenance with a very soft grow‑out
Ombre
  • Gradual shift from deeper roots to lighter ends
  • Can be subtle (sombre) or high‑contrast, fashion‑forward
  • Pairs beautifully with waves for visible gradient

Aftercare & maintenance

  • Use color‑safe, sulfate‑free shampoo/conditioner.
  • Gloss/toner refresh every 6–8 weeks to maintain tone.
  • Weekly hydration mask; bond‑building treatment if lightened.
  • Heat protectant before styling; limit heat for longevity.

FAQ

Is balayage damaging?
We use protective lighteners and adjust developer strength, often adding bond‑builders. Hand‑painting also avoids repeated overlap.
Balayage vs ombre — which should I choose?
Pick balayage for natural ribbons of light; choose ombre for a clearer light‑to‑dark gradient. We can combine both for the best of each.
How often do I need maintenance?
Most balayage clients visit every 10–16 weeks. Tone/gloss can be refreshed sooner to keep the shade perfect between lightening visits.
