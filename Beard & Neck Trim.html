Aftercare tips

  • Wash 2–3× per week with gentle beard wash to avoid dryness.
  • Hydrate daily with beard oil or balm; brush to distribute evenly.
  • Trim stray hairs at home between visits; avoid cutting the neckline too high.
  • Return every 2–4 weeks to maintain shape and blend with your haircut.

FAQ

How low should my neckline be?
Generally at the natural curve just above your Adam’s apple, keeping the angle smooth from ear to ear. We’ll map it for your face and growth.
Razor or clipper finish?
Razor gives the sharpest edge; clippers are quicker and gentler for sensitive skin. We’ll choose based on your skin and preference.
